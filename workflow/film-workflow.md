## 2) Current Findings — Seedance + Storyboard

### Seedance (initial findings)
- Seedance appears to position itself as an AI video generation model/platform (text-to-video and image-to-video).
- It emphasizes:
  - rapid prototyping of scenes
  - multi-shot consistency (character/style continuity claims)
  - cinematic motion + higher output quality claims
- Practical use for your workflow right now:
  - Create quick "look tests" from scene prompts before committing to full storyboard passes.
  - Generate visual tone references (lighting/style/mood) to align creative direction early.
  - Test alt versions of key scenes for pacing and camera language.

**Caution:** Most claims are marketing-side; we should validate with your own controlled tests (same prompt across multiple tools, compare coherence and controllability).

### Storyboard (initial findings)
- Storyboard and shot list should work together, not as substitutes.
- Storyboard = visual intention (composition, blocking, mood, movement idea).
- Shot list = production execution (lens, setup, movement, gear, schedule impact).
- Recommended flow:
  1. Script beat breakdown
  2. Storyboard pass (rough)
  3. Shot list extraction from storyboard
  4. Tech scout + revisions
  5. Final boards + production shot list

## 3) Suggested End-to-End Workflow (Feature Film)

1. **Script Lock (or near-lock)**
   - Freeze scene goals and emotional beats.

2. **Scene Intent Notes**
   - For each scene: objective, conflict, turning point, emotional temperature.

3. **Seedance Look Development**
   - Prompt visual directions by scene family (home, workplace, street, etc.).
   - Save top 2-3 style candidates per scene family.

4. **Storyboard Pass v1 (fast/rough)**
   - Focus on coverage logic + emotional framing, not polish.

5. **Shot List Draft v1**
   - Convert storyboard frames into a production table (shot #, size, angle, movement, lens idea, notes).

6. **Director/Cinematography Pass**
   - Remove redundant coverage, reinforce visual motifs, improve transitions.

7. **Storyboard Pass v2 (selective polish)**
   - Polish only critical sequences (complex blocking, action, emotional peaks).

8. **Production Constraints Pass**
   - Location, time of day, cast availability, budget, practical effects.

9. **Final Shot List + Schedule Inputs**
   - Lock what must be captured vs optional inserts.

10. **On-set Flex Rules**
   - Define what can change and what cannot (story-critical frames).
