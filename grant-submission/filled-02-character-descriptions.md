# Deliverable 2 — Character Descriptions (Draft Fill)

## Project
- Title: Silent Cradle (working title: Akuaba)
- Date/version: 2026-02-28 v1

## A) Lina Morrison
- Role in story: Protagonist; Edmonton teacher navigating recurrent pregnancy loss.
- Public persona vs private self: Competent and composed outwardly; internally anxious, ashamed, and increasingly fixated on control.
- Core wound/fear: Fear of permanent childlessness and social judgment; fear of meaninglessness after repeated loss.
- Motivation: Regain agency and hope through any available mechanism.
- Internal contradiction: Seeks healing but chooses secrecy/control that deepens isolation.
- Relationship dynamics: Marriage strain with Rob; tension and mirror/challenge dynamic with Elena.
- Arc: From fragile resilience to obsession-driven rupture, ending in a painful reckoning with relational cost.

## B) Rob Morrison
- Role in story: Lina’s husband; grounding force and relational counterpoint.
- Motivation: Preserve relationship stability while coping with his own grief.
- Key conflict with Lina: Wants transparency and practical support; resists escalating ritual dependence.
- Arc: From patient support to boundary assertion under emotional exhaustion.

## C) Elena
- Role in story: Key supporting relationship (friend/colleague role within story world).
- Function in dramatic structure: External pressure toward truth, accountability, and relational reality.
- Arc: From support/concern to confrontation as Lina’s behavior destabilizes trust.

## Character Relationship Map
- Lina <-> Rob: Intimacy under strain; love versus avoidant coping.
- Lina <-> Elena: Care versus discomfort; challenge to denial.
- Rob <-> Elena: Shared concern filtered through different boundaries.
