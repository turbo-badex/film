I am happy to advise that your application for funding regarding **Project No-CI-PS-00133418 "Akuaba"** has been awarded a **Project/Script Development Grant for the Pre-Development phase in the amount of $5,000 through the Alberta Media Fund.**

The Alberta Media Fund and the grants it provides continue to be an important part of ensuring the ongoing growth and success of Alberta's screen-based production sector. As such, I am thrilled to be able to provide development funding that continues to assist Albertans like you in bringing their ideas and stories to life. Please confirm your acceptance of this grant by email to culturalindustries@gov.ab.ca.

Should you have any questions regarding the grant, please contact the Alberta Media Fund at 780-422-8584 (toll-free by first dialing 310-0000) or culturalindustries@gov.ab.ca.
