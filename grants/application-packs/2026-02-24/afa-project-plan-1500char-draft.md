# AFA Project Plan Draft (<=1500 chars)

## Version A (balanced, ready to paste)
Silent Cradle (working title previously submitted as Akuaba) is a psychologically grounded feature drama set in Alberta. It follows Lina, an Edmonton teacher navigating recurrent pregnancy loss whose private coping rituals gradually disrupt her marriage, friendships, and professional life. The film combines naturalistic storytelling with restrained folkloric imagery to examine fertility grief, shame, and the pressure to perform “normalcy.”

This AFA request supports a distinct Phase 2: Advanced Script & Market-Readiness. Following earlier foundational development support, this phase focuses on non-overlapping work needed to bring the project to submission-ready standard: two rounds of dramaturgical/script-consultant feedback, targeted rewrite and polish, expanded cultural and mental-health advisor consultations, and a structured table-read refinement process. We will also complete a professional package set (logline, synopsis suite, director statement, and visual lookbook polish) for festival/market positioning.

The outcome will be a stronger, production-facing screenplay and clearer presentation materials for financing and festival pathways, while centering an Alberta-rooted story with cross-cultural relevance and meaningful social resonance.

## Version B (more commercial/festival emphasis)
Silent Cradle (working title previously submitted as Akuaba) is an Alberta-set feature drama with psychological tension and strong festival potential. The story follows Lina, an Edmonton teacher whose grief after recurrent pregnancy loss turns into escalating control and relational breakdown. Through a naturalistic style with subtle folkloric elements, the project addresses fertility, mental health, and cultural identity in a way designed to connect with both Canadian and international audiences.

This AFA application funds a non-overlapping Phase 2: Advanced Script & Market-Readiness. Activities include professional dramaturgical support, focused rewrite/polish, cultural and mental-health advisory input, and a formal table-read refinement pass. Deliverables include a production-facing screenplay draft plus a refined market package (logline, synopsis set, director statement, and lookbook/pitch polish).

By completion, the project will be materially stronger for financing conversations and targeted festival submissions, while continuing to develop Alberta talent and Alberta-centered storytelling.
