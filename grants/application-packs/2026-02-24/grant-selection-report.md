## 2) Most viable grants right now (selected)

## A) AFA — Film and Video Individual Project Funding (**PRIMARY / IMMEDIATE**)
- **Why selected now:** Best fit for current maturity and timeline; supports script/pre-production/production phases for individual Alberta artists.
- **Deadline:** **March 2, 2026** (next after that: Sep 1, 2026)
- **Amount:** Up to **$18,000**
- **Official source:** https://www.affta.ab.ca/funding/find-funding/film-and-video-individual-project-funding

## B) Alberta Made Production Grant (AMPG) (**PRIMARY / NEXT WINDOW PREP**)
- **Why selected now:** Strong Alberta fit for production-stage financing prep; next viable intake allows build time after AFA submission.
- **Deadline window:** **July 2–16, 2026**
- **Amount:** 25% of eligible Alberta production costs, up to **$125,000** (+ optional training/mentorship top-up up to $35,000)
- **Official sources:**
  - https://www.alberta.ca/alberta-made-production-grant
  - Guidelines hub: https://open.alberta.ca/publications/alberta-made-production-grant-program-guidelines

## C) Canada Council — Explore and Create / Artistic Creation (**SECONDARY / FLEXIBLE TIMING**)
- **Why selected now:** Ongoing intake language, useful for artist-led creation trajectory if AFA/AMPG timing or fit changes.
- **Deadline:** “Any time before the start date” language on program page (must verify in portal before final plan lock).
- **Official source:** https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants/explore-and-create
