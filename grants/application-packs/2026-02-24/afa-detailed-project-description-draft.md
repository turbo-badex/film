# AFA Detailed Project Description Draft

Project title: **Silent Cradle** (working title previously submitted as **Akuaba**)  
Category: **Art Production**  
Phase: **Phase 2 — Advanced Script & Market-Readiness**

## Project Overview
*Silent Cradle* is an Alberta-set feature drama (approx. 90 minutes) that follows Lina, an Edmonton teacher navigating the emotional aftermath of recurrent pregnancy loss. As Lina turns to private rituals for control and comfort, those coping strategies gradually strain her marriage, friendships, and professional life. The film blends grounded realism with restrained folkloric symbolism to explore grief, stigma, cultural identity, and the social pressure surrounding fertility.

The project is led from an Alberta perspective and is intended to contribute to culturally specific, socially relevant storytelling in the province. The creative intent is to avoid sensationalism and instead portray emotional and relational consequences with nuance, empathy, and psychological credibility.

## Purpose of This Funding Request
This AFA request supports a distinct, non-overlapping development phase focused on strengthening the screenplay and preparing professional market-facing materials. Foundational script development has already occurred. This phase advances the project from strong draft to production- and submission-ready package quality.

Specifically, this phase will fund:
1. Structured table-read execution and feedback capture.
2. Expanded cultural and mental-health advisory consultation.
3. Focused story-editing and continuity refinement.
4. Notes integration and script coordination support.
5. Preparation of polished market materials (logline set, synopsis suite, director statement, and lookbook/pitch package components).

## Activities and Deliverables
### 1) Script refinement workflow
- Run a structured table read with selected readers.
- Capture performance/clarity notes and identify pacing, dialogue, and emotional beat issues.
- Complete a targeted story-editing pass (line-level and continuity-level polish).
- Integrate revisions into an updated screenplay draft.

**Deliverable:** Revised screenplay draft suitable for production planning and external review.

### 2) Advisory integration
- Conduct consultation rounds with cultural advisors to maintain authenticity and avoid flattening cultural elements.
- Conduct consultation with mental-health-informed advisor(s) to ensure ethical and grounded portrayal of grief and psychological strain.
- Translate advisory feedback into actionable script updates.

**Deliverable:** Advisor-informed revision notes and implemented script changes.

### 3) Market-readiness package build
- Refine project logline and short/medium synopsis versions.
- Finalize director statement and artistic intent language.
- Build/polish visual lookbook and pitch-deck-ready narrative assets.
- Prepare submission-friendly project metadata and one-page summary materials.

**Deliverable:** Coherent written/visual package for financing and festival pathway conversations.

## Artistic and Community Value
The project addresses topics that are often underrepresented or simplified: fertility grief, reproductive pressure, and the social shame that can isolate individuals and families. Through an Alberta context and a Nigerian-Canadian lens, *Silent Cradle* aims to broaden local screen narratives and support audience reflection around care, identity, and resilience.

In development approach and eventual outreach strategy, the project prioritizes respectful consultation and responsible storytelling. The work is intended to resonate with both festival audiences and communities directly connected to fertility and mental-health experiences.
