# Silent Cradle — Artistic Intent (Draft)

## Core intent
To create a grounded psychological feature that explores infertility-related shame, social performance, and the cost of emotional avoidance within intimate relationships.

## Thematic pillars
1. **Control as self-protection** — how competence can become armor.
2. **Private grief vs public identity** — social scripts around motherhood and status.
3. **Relational consequence** — the moral stakes are interpersonal, not abstract.

## Story approach
- Realist tone with ambiguity around symbolic objects.
- Behavior-first scenes (tasks, routines, repeated gestures).
- Escalation via social pressure and institutional scrutiny.
- Ending with incomplete repair, not tidy redemption.

## Audience impact goal
- Encourage empathy without moral simplification.
- Spark conversation around hidden grief and partnership strain.
- Offer a culturally textured, contemporary Alberta-set character drama.
