# Silent Cradle — Director Statement (Draft)

I want to make a film about what people do when pain has no socially acceptable language.

In *Silent Cradle*, Lina is not a villain. She is intelligent, disciplined, and good at carrying weight in public. But she cannot metabolize private grief without controlling the world around her. That contradiction is the heart of the film.

Formally, I want the film to begin with routine and precision: classrooms, kitchen tasks, administrative language. As pressure rises, those routines become sites of emotional leakage. The camera should stay close to behavior and consequence rather than broad explanation. Dialogue is designed to carry subtext and restraint, with occasional dry humor to keep scenes human and breathable.

I’m influenced by character-driven moral dramas where conflict emerges from ordinary social situations, not spectacle. The aim is to create a tense but intimate portrait of a marriage under strain and a woman whose coping strategy slowly isolates her.

I do not want to provide easy absolution. The ending is intentionally unresolved in spiritual terms, but clear in relational terms: choosing image over vulnerability has a cost.
