# Silent Cradle — Sequence 4 (Act 2B) Scenes (v1)

Goal of sequence: create a credible opening for repair, then have Lina reject it at the midpoint by choosing image/control over vulnerability.

---

## Scene 1 — Dr. Chen offers a real exit

**INT. DR. CHEN’S OFFICE — LATE AFTERNOON**

Warm lamp. Tissue box untouched. A white-noise machine hums softly.

Lina sits upright, hands clasped, posture closer to a staff meeting than therapy.

DR. CHEN (40s) reads from intake notes, then closes the folder.

**DR. CHEN**
I’m not here to take hope away from you.

Lina gives a guarded nod.

**LINA**
Good.

**DR. CHEN**
I am here to ask whether your coping is reducing suffering... or reorganizing your life around fear.

A beat.

**LINA**
That sounds like a fancy way to call me irrational.

**DR. CHEN**
No.
(beat)
It’s a way to measure cost.

Lina looks away.

**DR. CHEN** (CONT'D)
Would you be willing to try one week of structured reset?
No object rituals after 9 PM.
One transparent check-in with Rob per night.
One day with no avoidance behaviors.

Lina almost laughs.

**LINA**
"No avoidance behaviors" isn’t medicine.

**DR. CHEN**
No. It’s data.
If your system helps, it survives contact with openness.
If it collapses, that tells us something too.

Lina takes this in. She’s moved, but defensive.

**DR. CHEN** (CONT'D)
You can keep dignity and still be honest.
Those are not enemies.

Long silence.

**LINA**
I’ll think about it.

**DR. CHEN**
Thinking is fine.
Just don’t call postponement "thinking."

Lina stands, nods once, exits.

## Scene 2 — Home opening (the repair path appears)

**INT. APARTMENT LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**

No TV. Rain at the window. Rob waits at the table with two mugs of tea.

Lina enters, sets keys down, sees he stayed up.

**ROB**
How did it go?

**LINA**
Fine.

He doesn’t move.

**ROB**
Fine like fine?
Or fine like "don’t ask me again"?

Lina sits. For once, no immediate deflection.

**LINA**
She asked me to do a one-week reset.

Rob leans forward, hopeful but careful.

**ROB**
Okay.

**LINA**
No rituals after nine.
Check-ins. Transparent schedule.

**ROB**
That sounds... possible.

Lina’s eyes fill, but she keeps her voice flat.

**LINA**
I don’t want to be managed.

**ROB**
I don’t want to manage you.
I want to stop losing you while you’re in the room.

Silence.

Lina takes a breath that could become honesty.

**LINA**
I am terrified all the time.

Rob nods, almost relieved to hear anything real.

**ROB**
I know.

**LINA**
No, you don’t.

The window closes. Lina retreats again.

**LINA** (CONT'D)
Tomorrow I have assembly duty at ten-thirty.
Don’t wait up.

Rob’s shoulders drop.

**ROB**
We were there for one second.

Lina stands.

**LINA**
I’m tired.

She exits to bedroom.

Rob sits with both mugs untouched.

## Scene 3 — Midpoint choice: image over repair

**INT. SCHOOL ADMIN OFFICE — NEXT MORNING**

Glass walls. Diplomas. Controlled quiet.

Vice Principal Singh and Elena sit across from Lina. Not a formal disciplinary meeting — but close.

A printed sheet on table: "Parent Feedback Themes — Communication Tone."

**SINGH**
This is not punitive.
We need alignment before this escalates.

Lina scans the sheet.

Quoted comments:
- "Too harsh."
- "My child feels judged."
- "Feedback lacks warmth."

**ELENA**
I told them your standards are strong.
This is about packaging.

Lina sets the sheet down.

**LINA**
So we’re grading my tone now.

**SINGH**
We’re protecting classroom trust.

**LINA**
By asking me to sound less honest.

**ELENA**
No. By asking you to sound reachable.

A long beat. This is the fork.

Singh slides a draft support option:
- temporary load adjustment
- optional coaching observation
- wellness leave resources

**SINGH**
Take this and we reset cleanly.
No notes on your file if we do it now.

Lina stares at the page. This is the open door.

Then she pushes it back.

**LINA**
I’m declining all of it.

**ELENA**
Lina—

**LINA**
I am fully capable of doing my job.
I don’t need a fragility plan.

**SINGH**
Then we document this meeting and proceed formally if concerns continue.

Lina nods, jaw set.

**LINA**
Do what you need.

She stands to leave, then turns back.

**LINA** (CONT'D)
For the record, precision is not cruelty.

She exits.

**INT. SCHOOL WASHROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Lina locks a stall, sits on closed lid, phone in hand.

Draft text to Rob:
*I’m trying. I’m scared. I don’t know how to stop controlling everything.*

She stares at it.

Deletes.

New text:
*Running late. Don’t wait for me.*

Send.

She presses palms to her eyes, breathes, then composes herself.

Unlocks stall. Neutral face restored.

**END SEQUENCE 4 (MIDPOINT)**

## Sequence 4 outcome check
- Repair path was concrete and credible (Dr. Chen + Rob + admin reset option).
- Lina had multiple off-ramps and refused all three.
- Midpoint turn: story shifts from "can she recover quickly?" to "what damage will this control strategy now cause?"
- Institutional track moves from concern to documentable process.
