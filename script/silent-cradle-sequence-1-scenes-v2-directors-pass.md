## Scene 1 — Classroom competence

**INT. ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — LATE AFTERNOON**

Coats zip. Chairs scrape. Lina writes on the board in neat block letters.

**LINA**
Last thing. If you quote, cite. If you claim, prove. No shortcuts.

A STUDENT (10) raises a hand.

**STUDENT**
Miss, does "I just felt like it" count as proof?

A ripple of laughter.

**LINA**
It counts as honesty.
(beat)
Not proof.

Bell. Kids flood out.

Lina sits. Reflection papers. Red pen uncapped.

Quick marks:
- "too broad"
- "unclear"
- "show me"

Elena appears at the door with a mug.

**ELENA**
You’re still here.

**LINA**
I work here.

**ELENA**
Friday, staff shower. I put you on setup. You want out, say it.

Lina keeps marking.

**LINA**
It’s fine.

**ELENA**
Okay.

Elena lingers, then leaves.

Lina reads one student line: *"Sometimes adults pretend not to be sad so kids don’t worry."*

She writes "vague." Stops. Crosses it out.

Closes the folder.

**ALT LINE OPTION (Lina to Elena):**
- "Leave me on it."
- "I’ll be there."

## Scene 2 — Domestic procedure

**INT. LINA & ROB’S APARTMENT — EVENING**

Small kitchen. Fridge hum. Knife on cutting board.

Rob chops cucumbers with quiet focus.

Lina enters, hangs coat, washes hands immediately.

**ROB**
Day okay?

**LINA**
Nobody cried. That’s a win.

She sorts mail into precise stacks.

**ROB**
Elena said Friday runs late. Want pickup?

**LINA**
I can get home.

**ROB**
Not what I asked.

A beat.

**LINA**
Then yes.

He nods.

**ROB**
Clinic called.

Her hand freezes over an envelope.

**ROB** (CONT'D)
Tuesday. 10:40.

**LINA**
It was 9:30.

**ROB**
I know.

**LINA**
I moved prep for 9:30.

**ROB**
Move it again.

**LINA**
Last time I moved it, admin flagged it.

Rob sets the knife down.

**ROB**
Can we do this without hiding in logistics?

Silence.

**LINA**
Logistics are how things stay upright.

**ROB**
Not us.

He resumes chopping, slower.

**ROB** (CONT'D)
My mom asked about Sunday.

Lina keeps aligning envelopes.

**LINA**
No.

**ROB**
You sure?

**LINA**
Yes.

She taps the mail stack straight, edge to edge.

**ALT LINE OPTION (Rob):**
- "Schedules aren’t feelings."
- "You keep giving me admin updates instead of answers."

## Scene 3 — Social trigger / first cost

**INT. SCHOOL GYM — FRIDAY EVENING**

Streamers. Folding tables. Parent chatter. Kids' drawings on a wall labeled: "MY FAMILY."

Lina places a tray of cupcakes near a display.

A PARENT VOLUNTEER smiles.

**PARENT VOLUNTEER**
Great — put yours by the baby photos.

Lina glances: sonograms, newborn shots, cards that read "Our little miracle."

She places the tray down carefully.

A STAFF MEMBER waves from a photo booth backdrop.

**STAFF MEMBER**
Lina! Future moms photo — come on!

Light laughter. Casual. Unthinking.

Lina gives her work-smile.

**LINA**
One minute.

Phone buzz: **ROB CALLING**.

She silences it.

Elena approaches.

**ELENA**
Want air?

**LINA**
I’m good.

**ELENA**
You can leave. Nobody’s keeping score.

**LINA**
I helped run this.

Over Elena’s shoulder, a child pins a drawing: mother + child, huge red crayon heart.

Lina steps out.

**INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS**

Fluorescent lights. Empty corridor.

Texts from Rob:
- *You okay?*
- *I’m outside in 10.*
- *Lina?*

She types: *All good. Finishing up.*

Stops. Deletes.

Types: *Don’t come. I’ll handle it.*

Send.

She leans against the wall. One breath too fast. Then slower.

Wipes under one eye with the heel of her palm.

Back to neutral.

She walks toward the gym.

At the doorway: the same composed smile returns.

**ALT LINE OPTION (Elena):**
- "You don’t owe this room your face."
- "If you need ten minutes, take ten."

**END SEQUENCE 1**

## Sequence 1 outcome check
- Lina chooses presentation over vulnerability in all three scenes.
- Rob is kept outside her interior world.
- Public shame trigger lands without melodrama.
- Immediate cost: controlled behavior deepens isolation.
