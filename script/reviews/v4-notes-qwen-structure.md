## 2) The core structural problem

**The yearning is never dramatized before the ending.** V3's Act 1 establishes Lina's *control* (competence, logistics, avoidance) but not the *want*. The title — Silent Cradle, an unborn child — is not dramatized in Act 1 at all. The audience meets the grief secondhand: "the clinic," "Tuesday 10:40," a canceled appointment. Per D1, the loss must drive her interactions and thoughts *on screen from scene one*.

**Fix: the Act 1 break (scene 6, the cancellation) becomes the reveal.** When Lina cancels the Tuesday appointment, the phone screen names it — e.g., appointment title reads "L. Morrison — embryo transfer — confirmed." No lecture, no diagnosis, no medical exposition: one line of text on screen, then her thumb hovers, then *canceled*. The act break stops being "she avoids logistics" and becomes "she cancels her own hope to keep her system running." The sonogram pamphlet in the ending box (scene 24) then lands as a callback, not a first mention.
- Everything before it stays behavior-only (the gaze in scene 1, "My Family" wall in scene 3) — the reveal pays for all of it.
- *Open for @badex:* is an active-treatment framing (mid-cycle) acceptable, or should the screen stay vaguer ("fertility clinic — Tue 10:40")? Both work; the specific version is stronger if we keep it.

## 4) Key structural moves (detail)

### 4.1 The reveal (scene 6)
See §2. Consequence chain: the reveal also retro-motivates scene 2 (Rob's "Tuesday. 10:40" — he *knows what it is*, which deepens "Not us.") and makes the coda pamphlet a full-circle object.

### 4.2 The shop's causal bridge (Codex #2)
Options, ranked: (a) Lina's research habit — she's been quietly researching (her "no shortcuts" ethos applied to the thing she's most afraid of): the shop's back-card reputation is the last thing her googling led her to. **Recommended** — it's on-character and costs zero runtime. (b) Elena mentions the shop in passing. (c) A student's family connection. (a) also gives the consultant a clear question: is a researched, deliberate first visit more credible/respectful than an impulse?

### 4.3 Kwame's second entry (D4)
Act II-B placement, after the formal process begins and before the rupture — Lina at her lowest control. Function: the **culture answers the institution's question** ("is this a problem?") with a different framework ("this is an object; what are *you* doing to it?"). He is the only character who can tell the truth without a file on her. Hard rule: no lecturing, no magic, no retrieval-of-the-object climax. ⚑ Full consultant pressure-test (brief question 14).

### 4.4 Compressing the workplace machinery (D6, Codex #4)
V3's scenes 12→16 is one escalation repeated four times. V4 keeps: midpoint fork (12), one conference (15), one compressed formal-process beat (18), then straight to the hearing. Recovered budget funds the three NEW character scenes (13, 14, 19) — the character-investment north star.

### 4.5 Recovery with time (D7)
Six weeks, on-screen, with one relapse (scene 25). The imperfect attempt is what separates "administratively resolved" from "earned."
