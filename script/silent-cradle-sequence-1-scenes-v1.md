# Silent Cradle — Sequence 1 (Act 1A) Scenes (v1)

Goal of sequence: establish Lina’s public competence mask, private wound, and first emotional cost.

---

## Scene 1 — Classroom competence

**INT. ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — LATE AFTERNOON**

Students are packing up. Coats zip. Chairs scrape. Lina stands at the board, calm and efficient.

**LINA**
Before you run, one thing. If you use a quote, you cite it. If you make a claim, you back it up. No shortcuts.

A few kids nod. One STUDENT (10) raises a hand.

**STUDENT**
Miss, is "I just felt like it" a claim?

A few kids laugh.

Lina almost smiles.

**LINA**
In this class? It’s a confession.

Bell. Kids spill out.

Lina sits, opens a stack of reflections. Red pen uncapped.

She marks fast:
- "too broad"
- "unclear"
- "proof?"

ELENA (mid-30s, warm but watchful) appears at the door with a mug.

**ELENA**
You’re still grading yesterday’s set?

**LINA**
Today’s.

**ELENA**
You know we’re not paid by the red mark.

**LINA**
Could’ve fooled me.

Elena steps in, lowers her voice.

**ELENA**
Hey... staff shower is Friday. I put your name down for setup. You good with that?

Lina keeps marking.

**LINA**
Sure.

**ELENA**
You don’t have to, if it’s weird.

Lina pauses one beat, then circles a sentence hard.

**LINA**
It’s not weird.

Elena reads the room, nods once, exits.

Lina stares at a student line: *"Sometimes adults pretend not to be sad so kids don’t worry."*

She writes "vague"... stops... scratches it out.

She closes the folder without finishing.

## Scene 2 — Domestic procedure

**INT. LINA & ROB’S APARTMENT — EVENING**

Small condo kitchen. Soft hum of fridge. Rob chops cucumbers with careful precision.

Lina enters, hangs coat, washes hands immediately.

**ROB**
How was school?

**LINA**
No fire. No lawsuits. Good day.

She opens mail, sorts into neat piles.

Rob watches her for a second.

**ROB**
Elena texted. Friday thing at school. You want me to pick you up after?

**LINA**
I can Uber.

**ROB**
That wasn’t the question.

She keeps sorting.

**LINA**
Then yes. Pick me up.

Rob nods. Beat.

**ROB**
Clinic called.

Lina’s hands stop over an envelope.

**ROB** (CONT'D)
They moved our slot. Tuesday. 10:40.

**LINA**
We had 9:30.

**ROB**
I know.

**LINA**
I took prep first block off for 9:30.

**ROB**
You can switch it.

**LINA**
Last time I switched, Patel covered and admin made a note.

Rob puts down the knife.

**ROB**
Can we do this one without turning it into logistics?

Long beat.

**LINA**
Logistics are why things don’t collapse.

**ROB**
That’s not true and you know it.

He resumes chopping, slower.

**ROB** (CONT'D)
My mom called.

Lina says nothing.

**ROB** (CONT'D)
She asked if we’re coming Sunday.

**LINA**
No.

**ROB**
Lina—

**LINA**
I said no.

She aligns the mail stack perfectly, edge to edge.

## Scene 3 — Social trigger / first cost

**INT. SCHOOL GYM — FRIDAY EVENING**

Staff event. Streamers, folding tables, paper plates. Children’s drawings taped along one wall: "MY FAMILY." 

Lina carries a tray of cupcakes to a table marked "FAMILY BOARD."

A PARENT VOLUNTEER (friendly, oblivious) smiles.

**PARENT VOLUNTEER**
Perfect timing. Can you put yours by the baby photos?

Lina glances: newborn shots, sonograms, "Our little miracle" cards.

She sets the tray down anyway.

Another STAFF MEMBER waves at her from near a photo booth.

**STAFF MEMBER**
Lina! Come do one with us — "future moms" corner!

A few people laugh lightly, not cruelly, just automatic.

Lina smiles a work-smile.

**LINA**
In a minute.

Her phone buzzes: **ROB CALLING**.

She ignores it.

Elena approaches, reading Lina’s face.

**ELENA**
You okay?

**LINA**
I’m fine.

**ELENA**
You don’t have to stay.

**LINA**
I helped organize. I’m staying.

Over Elena’s shoulder, Lina sees a kid pin a drawing to the board: a mother with a child. Bold red crayon heart.

Her jaw tightens. She steps away toward the exit hall.

**INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS**

Fluorescent lights. Empty, quiet.

Rob is now texting:
- *You good?*
- *I’m outside in 10.*
- *Lina?*

Lina types: *All good. Finishing up.*

She doesn’t send it. Deletes.

Instead she types: *Don’t come. I’ll handle it.*

Sends.

She leans against the wall, closes her eyes, breathes through it.

Then she straightens, wipes under one eye quickly, and walks back toward the gym.

At the doorway she puts on the same composed smile.

**END SEQUENCE 1**

## Sequence 1 outcome check
- Lina chooses composure/performance over vulnerability in all three scenes.
- Rob is pushed further outside the emotional room.
- First social shame trigger lands in public context.
- Immediate cost: increased isolation masked as control.
