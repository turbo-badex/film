# Silent Cradle — Red Pen Sequence (V1)

## Scene 1 — CONTROL

**INT. CLASSROOM — LATE AFTERNOON**

Empty room. Winter light. Papers spread across Lina’s desk.

She marks fast with a red pen.

On page after page:
- "unclear"
- "too broad"
- "rewrite"

A TEACHER (40s) appears at the doorway with a bag on one shoulder.

**TEACHER**
You live here now?

Lina doesn’t look up.

**LINA**
Just catching up.

The teacher steps in, glances at the pile.

**TEACHER**
That many mistakes, huh?

**LINA**
That many shortcuts.

The teacher gives her a look — half smile, half concern.

**TEACHER**
You grade like somebody who never gets tired.

**LINA**
I get tired. I still do the work.

The teacher nods and leaves.

Lina circles one final sentence so hard the paper almost tears.

Cut to:

**INT. LINA’S APARTMENT — NIGHT**

Small kitchen. Quiet. Her partner, SAMIR, washes a cup at the sink.

Lina enters, drops her bag, starts sorting mail without opening it.

**SAMIR**
Can we talk for five minutes?

**LINA**
About?

**SAMIR**
Don’t do that.

She keeps sorting.

**LINA**
I’m not doing anything.

**SAMIR**
Exactly.

Long beat.

**LINA**
I don’t have energy for a fight.

**SAMIR**
I’m not asking for a fight.

**LINA**
Then not tonight.

She puts one envelope in a neat stack, perfectly aligned.

Cut.

## Scene 2 — FRACTURE

**INT. CLASSROOM — DAY**

Students writing silently. Lina sits with reflection essays.

She reads one line:

> "I kept saying I was fine so no one would panic."

Her red pen hovers.

She writes: "too emotional"

Stops.

Crosses it out.

Writes instead:

> "What part was hardest to say?"

A hand goes up. NORA (16), sharp, direct.

**NORA**
In the story, why didn’t she just tell the truth right away?

Lina looks up. The room waits.

**LINA**
What do you think?

**NORA**
Because she was scared.

**STUDENT 2 (O.S.)**
Or because she wanted control.

A few students murmur.

**LINA**
Could be both.

**NORA**
That sounds like avoiding the question.

Lina almost smiles.

**LINA**
Sometimes both is the answer people hate most.

Bell rings.

Students pack up.

Nora lingers, watches Lina close the essay folder.

Cut to:

**INT. APARTMENT — LATE NIGHT**

No TV. No music. Just the hum of the fridge.

Samir sits at the table. Lina stands, arms crossed.

**SAMIR**
You keep saying you were protecting us.

**LINA**
I was.

**SAMIR**
From what?

She doesn’t answer.

**SAMIR** (CONT'D)
From the truth? Or from losing control of how I’d see you?

Lina exhales. Small crack.

**LINA**
I didn’t know how to tell it without blowing everything up.

**SAMIR**
So you told me nothing.

He gets up, leaves the table.

Lina stays still, then sits in his chair.

Cut.

---

## Scene 3 — INTEGRATION

**INT. CLASSROOM — MORNING**

Lighter room. Less noise in Lina’s movements.

She returns graded papers.

There are still red marks, but fewer.

More comments now:
- "Good risk here."
- "Be precise, but stay honest."
- "Truth without cruelty."

Nora approaches after class, holding her paper.

**NORA**
Did I go too far in this one?

**LINA**
No.

**NORA**
It felt... exposed.

Lina nods.

**LINA**
That usually means it’s real.

Nora scans Lina’s comment.

**NORA**
"Truth without cruelty." What does that even mean?

Lina thinks before answering.

**LINA**
It means you don’t lie.
(beat)
And you don’t use truth like a weapon.

Nora nods, leaves.

Lina looks at the red pen in her hand.

Cut to:

**INT. APARTMENT — EVENING**

Same table as before. No sorting, no distractions.

Lina sits first. Samir stands in the doorway, cautious.

**LINA**
I don’t want to keep editing this like it’s homework.

He takes a seat, guarded.

**SAMIR**
Okay.

**LINA**
I’m going to tell you the truth.
Not to win. Not to defend myself.
Just... the truth.

He studies her.

**SAMIR**
Can you do that without grading me while I react?

Lina almost laughs — painful, honest.

She places the red pen in the center of the table.

**LINA**
I’ll try.

They sit in silence. Not fixed. But facing each other.

Fade out.
