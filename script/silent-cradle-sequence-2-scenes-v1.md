# Silent Cradle — Sequence 2 (Act 1B) Scenes (v1)

Goal of sequence: introduce akuaba through grounded context, show emotional relief, and end with Lina’s first lock-in choice (object over relational openness).

---

## Scene 1 — The shop (entry without mysticism)

**INT. CULTURAL ARTIFACT SHOP — LATE AFTERNOON**

Narrow aisles. Wood shelves. Brass bowls. Handwritten tags. Quiet instrumental radio in the back.

Lina stands near a shelf of carved figures. She isn’t browsing like a tourist; she’s reading labels.

KWAME (50s), measured, observant, appears beside her with no sales urgency.

**KWAME**
If you want the story, read the back card first.

Lina flips a card. A small carved figure: flat circular head, compact body.

**LINA**
Akuaba.

**KWAME**
Mm.

**LINA**
Fertility symbol?

**KWAME**
Among other things.
(beat)
People simplify it.

Lina keeps reading.

**LINA**
Do people buy it for... belief?

**KWAME**
People buy it for many reasons.
Comfort. Tradition. Memory.
Sometimes hope.

Lina almost puts it back. Doesn’t.

**LINA**
And you? What do you think it does?

Kwame shrugs lightly.

**KWAME**
I think objects don’t do much by themselves.
People do.

He gestures to the card.

**KWAME** (CONT'D)
If you take it, don’t make it a trick.
Treat it with respect, or leave it here.

Lina nods. Pays cash.

Kwame wraps it carefully in plain paper — not gift-style, not ceremonial.

**KWAME**
No shortcuts.

Lina registers the phrase.

**LINA**
I teach. I hate shortcuts.

She takes the wrapped object and leaves.

## Scene 2 — Relief ritual / first intimacy offer

**INT. APARTMENT BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Low lamp. Quiet house. Lina unwraps the akuaba and sets it on her dresser, then repositions it twice.

She sits on the edge of the bed, finally still.

Her breathing slows.

Rob watches from the doorway, careful not to spook the moment.

**ROB**
Where’d you get it?

**LINA**
A shop near 17th.

**ROB**
What is it?

**LINA**
Akuaba.

He nods, waiting.

**ROB**
And?

**LINA**
And nothing.
(beat)
I like looking at it.

Rob steps in, sits beside her.

**ROB**
You look calmer.

Lina gives a small shrug.

**LINA**
Maybe I’m tired.

He reaches for her hand. She lets him, but only briefly.

**ROB**
Can we actually talk tonight?
About Tuesday. About all of it.

Lina keeps her eyes on the object.

**LINA**
Not right now.

**ROB**
There’s never a right now.

She stands, adjusts the akuaba angle by a few centimeters.

**LINA**
I said not tonight.

Rob exhales, gets up.

**ROB**
Okay.

He leaves the room. Lina remains, facing the dresser.

After a long beat, she whispers — unsure if to herself or the object.

**LINA**
I just need one quiet week.

## Scene 3 — School pressure + lock-in decision (Act 1 break)

**INT. SCHOOL STAFF ROOM — MORNING**

Microwave hum. Coffee machine hiss. Bulletin board with event notices.

Lina enters with a container. Elena and two staff members are mid-conversation.

The tone shifts half a notch when Lina appears.

**STAFF #1**
Morning.

**ELENA**
Hey. Admin moved Tuesday PLC. You’ll still be there, right?

Lina opens the fridge.

**LINA**
What time?

**ELENA**
Ten-thirty.

Lina freezes for a second.

**LINA**
I have an appointment.

**ELENA**
With who?

**LINA**
Medical.

Elena nods, trying to be practical.

**ELENA**
Then tell Singh now. She’s already edgy about absences this month.

Staff #2 jumps in, trying to lighten.

**STAFF #2**
Tell her it’s for a noble cause.

A tiny laugh from someone else. Lina doesn’t join.

**ELENA**
Ignore him.
(softer)
Do you want me to cover first ten minutes?

Lina looks at her. Then at the family-photo mug on the table. Then away.

**LINA**
No. I’ll handle it.

**INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS**

Lina stands outside ADMIN OFFICE door with her phone.

On screen: **Clinic Confirmed — Tuesday 10:40**

She opens text thread with clinic.

Types: *Need to keep this slot. It’s important.*
Deletes.

Types: *Can you move me to next available after 4 PM?*
Stops.

Deletes.

She opens a new note app instead:
- Tuesday 9:15 — class prep
- 10:30 — PLC
- 12:10 — parent call

She closes the note.

Opens text to clinic again.

Types: *I can’t make Tuesday. Please cancel.*

She stares at it. Sends.

Phone screen: **Appointment canceled.**

Lina locks phone, slips it into pocket.

She stands there, jaw tight, then heads toward class.

**END SEQUENCE 2 (ACT 1 BREAK)**
