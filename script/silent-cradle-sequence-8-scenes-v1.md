# Silent Cradle — Sequence 8 (Act 3B) Scenes (v1)

Goal of sequence: deliver ambiguous spiritual/object ending with clear relational lesson and altered trajectory (not neat closure).

---

## Scene 1 — Return to classroom (changed behavior, not speeches)

**INT. ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — MORNING**

Same room, same desks, softer energy.

Lina distributes revised writing assignments. Her tone is measured, less sharp.

**LINA**
Same standards. Different process.
If you make a strong point, I’ll ask for evidence.
If you get stuck, I’ll ask a better question.

Maya (from Seq 6) watches Lina carefully.

**MAYA**
So... we can still be wrong?

A few kids snicker.

**LINA**
Please be wrong.
That means you’re trying.

Lina moves desk to desk during writing time.

On one paper she writes in red:
- "Good risk"
- "Be specific"

She pauses, then adds in smaller script:
- "Don’t hide your claim."

She doesn’t cross it out this time.

At the board she accidentally reaches for a black marker, then changes to red. Holds it for a beat, then sets it down and keeps teaching without writing anything.

No one comments. The room breathes.

## Scene 2 — Threshold encounter with Rob (not reconciled)

**EXT. APARTMENT BUILDING ENTRY — DUSK**

Snowmelt in gutters. Dim porch light.

Lina arrives carrying groceries and sees Rob at the doorway with a small storage box.

Inside the box: books, a mug, old clinic paperwork.

They stand with awkward familiarity.

**ROB**
These were still at my sister’s.
Thought you’d want them.

Lina sets grocery bag down, takes the box.

**LINA**
Thanks.

A beat.

**ROB**
How was school?

**LINA**
Quieter.
(beat)
Harder in a better way.

Rob nods.

**ROB**
I heard from Elena.
She said you apologized to the class.

**LINA**
I corrected myself.

He almost smiles.

**ROB**
That sounds like your version of an apology.

Lina gives a tiny, tired smile back.

**LINA**
Probably.

Silence.

**ROB**
I’m not ready to move back.

**LINA**
I know.

**ROB**
I’m also not... gone.

Lina absorbs this without grabbing at it.

**LINA**
Okay.

He looks at the grocery bag.

**ROB**
You need help carrying that?

Lina shakes her head.

**LINA**
I’ve got it.

He nods, steps back.

**ROB**
Goodnight, Lina.

**LINA**
Night.

He walks away.

Lina watches him go, then picks up the bag and box, enters building.

## Scene 3 — Ambiguous coda (object unresolved, cost clear)

**INT. APARTMENT BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Low lamp. Quiet. No score.

Lina opens the storage box on the bed.

Inside: clinic forms, old sonogram pamphlet, a photo strip of her and Rob laughing years ago.

She places the photo strip on dresser.

The akuaba sits nearby.

She picks up the object, wipes a thumb across its face as if cleaning dust—or comforting herself.

She sets it down.

Then moves it away from center, to the side of the dresser.

Not hidden. Not shrine-like.

She opens a notebook and writes one line:

> "Truth without cruelty."

She stares at the words.

Tears rise, but she stays composed.

Her phone buzzes.
Message from unknown parent number:
*"Maya came home proud today. Thank you for seeing her."*

Lina reads it twice.

She looks at the akuaba.

Long, unreadable gaze — gratitude? dependence? farewell? all three?

She turns off the lamp.

In darkness, city light from the window outlines both Lina and the object in the same frame.

Hold.

Cut to black.

**END SEQUENCE 8 / END FILM**

- Rob/Lina relationship remains unresolved but not severed.
- Akuaba meaning remains ambiguous (comfort, belief, symbol, or unresolved attachment).
- Moral clarity stands: image-control cost Lina key relationships; recovery is possible but costly and incomplete.
