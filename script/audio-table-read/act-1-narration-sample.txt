SILENT CRADLE
Feature screenplay draft — V3 Polish




Interior ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

Coats zip. Chairs scrape. Lina writes on the board in neat block letters.


Lina:
Last thing. If you quote, cite. If you claim, prove. No shortcuts.

A STUDENT (10) raises a hand.


Student:
Miss, does "I just felt like it" count as proof?

A ripple of laughter.


Lina:
It counts as honesty.
(beat)
Not proof.

Bell. Kids flood out.

Lina sits. Reflection papers. Red pen uncapped.

Quick marks:
- "too broad"
- "unclear"
- "show me"

Elena appears at the door with a mug.


Elena:
You’re still here.


Lina:
I work here.


Elena:
Friday, staff shower. I put you on setup. You want out, say it.

Lina keeps marking.


Lina:
It’s fine.


Elena:
Okay.

Elena lingers, then leaves.

Lina reads one student line: "Sometimes adults pretend not to be sad so kids don’t worry."

She writes "vague." Stops. Crosses it out.

Closes the folder.


Interior LINA & ROB’S APARTMENT — EVENING

Small kitchen. Fridge hum. Knife on cutting board.

Rob chops cucumbers with quiet focus.

Lina enters, hangs coat, washes hands immediately.


Rob:
Day okay?


Lina:
Nobody cried. That’s a win.

She sorts mail into precise stacks.


Rob:
Elena said Friday runs late. Want pickup?


Lina:
I can get home.


Rob:
Not what I asked.

A beat.


Lina:
Then yes.

He nods.


Rob:
Clinic called.

Her hand freezes over an envelope.

ROB (CONT'D)
Tuesday. 10:40.


Lina:
It was 9:30.


Rob:
I know.


Lina:
I moved prep for 9:30.


Rob:
Move it again.


Lina:
Last time I moved it, admin flagged it.

Rob sets the knife down.


Rob:
Can we do this without hiding in logistics?

Silence.


Lina:
Logistics are how things stay upright.


Rob:
Not us.

He resumes chopping, slower.

ROB (CONT'D)
My mom asked about Sunday.

Lina keeps aligning envelopes.


Lina:
No.


Rob:
You sure?


Lina:
Yes.

She taps the mail stack straight, edge to edge.


Interior SCHOOL GYM — FRIDAY EVENING

Streamers. Folding tables. Parent chatter. Kids' drawings on a wall labeled: "MY FAMILY."

Lina places a tray of cupcakes near a display.

A PARENT VOLUNTEER smiles.


Parent Volunteer:
Great — put yours by the baby photos.

Lina glances: sonograms, newborn shots, cards that read "Our little miracle."

She places the tray down carefully.

A STAFF MEMBER waves from a photo booth backdrop.


Staff Member:
Lina! Future moms photo — come on!

Light laughter. Casual. Unthinking.

Lina gives her work-smile.


Lina:
One minute.

Phone buzz: ROB CALLING.

She silences it.

Elena approaches.


Elena:
Want air?


Lina:
I’m good.
