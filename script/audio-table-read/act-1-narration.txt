SILENT CRADLE
Feature screenplay draft — V3 Polish

Scene 1: Classroom Competence

Interior ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

Coats zip. Chairs scrape. Lina writes on the board in neat block letters.

Lina:
Last thing. If you quote, cite. If you claim, prove. No shortcuts.

A STUDENT (10) raises a hand.

Student:
Miss, does "I just felt like it" count as proof?

A ripple of laughter.

Lina:
It counts as honesty.
(beat)
Not proof.

Bell. Kids flood out.

Lina sits. Reflection papers. Red pen uncapped.

Quick marks:
- "too broad"
- "unclear"
- "show me"

Elena appears at the door with a mug.

Elena:
You’re still here.

Lina:
I work here.

Elena:
Friday, staff shower. I put you on setup. You want out, say it.

Lina keeps marking.

Lina:
It’s fine.

Elena:
Okay.

Elena lingers, then leaves.

Lina reads one student line: "Sometimes adults pretend not to be sad so kids don’t worry."

She writes "vague." Stops. Crosses it out.

Closes the folder.

Scene 2: Domestic Procedure

Interior LINA & ROB’S APARTMENT — EVENING

Small kitchen. Fridge hum. Knife on cutting board.

Rob chops cucumbers with quiet focus.

Lina enters, hangs coat, washes hands immediately.

Rob:
Day okay?

Lina:
Nobody cried. That’s a win.

She sorts mail into precise stacks.

Rob:
Elena said Friday runs late. Want pickup?

Lina:
I can get home.

Rob:
Not what I asked.

A beat.

Lina:
Then yes.

He nods.

Rob:
Clinic called.

Her hand freezes over an envelope.

ROB (CONT'D)
Tuesday. 10:40.

Lina:
It was 9:30.

Rob:
I know.

Lina:
I moved prep for 9:30.

Rob:
Move it again.

Lina:
Last time I moved it, admin flagged it.

Rob sets the knife down.

Rob:
Can we do this without hiding in logistics?

Silence.

Lina:
Logistics are how things stay upright.

Rob:
Not us.

He resumes chopping, slower.

ROB (CONT'D)
My mom asked about Sunday.

Lina keeps aligning envelopes.

Lina:
No.

Rob:
You sure?

Lina:
Yes.

She taps the mail stack straight, edge to edge.

Scene 3: Social Trigger / First Cost

Interior SCHOOL GYM — FRIDAY EVENING

Streamers. Folding tables. Parent chatter. Kids' drawings on a wall labeled: "MY FAMILY."

Lina places a tray of cupcakes near a display.

A PARENT VOLUNTEER smiles.

Parent Volunteer:
Great — put yours by the baby photos.

Lina glances: sonograms, newborn shots, cards that read "Our little miracle."

She places the tray down carefully.

A STAFF MEMBER waves from a photo booth backdrop.

Staff Member:
Lina! Future moms photo — come on!

Light laughter. Casual. Unthinking.

Lina gives her work-smile.

Lina:
One minute.

Phone buzz: ROB CALLING.

She silences it.

Elena approaches.

Elena:
Want air?

Lina:
I’m good.

Elena:
You can leave. Nobody’s keeping score.

Lina:
I helped run this.

Over Elena’s shoulder, a child pins a drawing: mother + child, huge red crayon heart.

Lina steps out.

Interior SCHOOL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS

Fluorescent lights. Empty corridor.

Texts from Rob:
- You okay?
- I’m outside in 10.
- Lina?

She types: All good. Finishing up.

Stops. Deletes.

Types: Don’t come. I’ll handle it.

Send.

She leans against the wall. One breath too fast. Then slower.

Wipes under one eye with the heel of her palm.

Back to neutral.

She walks toward the gym.

At the doorway: the same composed smile returns.

Scene 4: The Shop (Entry Without Mysticism)

Interior CULTURAL ARTIFACT SHOP — LATE AFTERNOON

Narrow aisles. Wood shelves. Brass bowls. Handwritten tags. Quiet instrumental radio in the back.

Lina stands near a shelf of carved figures. She isn’t browsing like a tourist; she’s reading labels.

KWAME (50s), measured, observant, appears beside her with no sales urgency.

Kwame:
If you want the story, read the back card first.

Lina flips a card. A small carved figure: flat circular head, compact body.

Lina:
Akuaba.

Kwame:
Mm.

Lina:
Fertility symbol?

Kwame:
Among other things.
(beat)
People simplify it.

Lina keeps reading.

Lina:
Do people buy it for... belief?

Kwame:
People buy it for many reasons.
Comfort. Tradition. Memory.
Sometimes hope.

Lina almost puts it back. Doesn’t.

Lina:
And you? What do you think it does?

Kwame shrugs lightly.

Kwame:
I think objects don’t do much by themselves.
People do.

He gestures to the card.

KWAME (CONT'D)
If you take it, don’t make it a trick.
Treat it with respect, or leave it here.

Lina nods. Pays cash.

Kwame wraps it carefully in plain paper — not gift-style, not ceremonial.

Kwame:
No shortcuts.

Lina registers the phrase.

Lina:
I teach. I hate shortcuts.

She takes the wrapped object and leaves.

Scene 5: Relief Ritual / First Intimacy Offer

Interior APARTMENT BEDROOM — NIGHT

Low lamp. Quiet house. Lina unwraps the akuaba and sets it on her dresser, then repositions it twice.

She sits on the edge of the bed, finally still.

Her breathing slows.

Rob watches from the doorway, careful not to spook the moment.

Rob:
Where’d you get it?

Lina:
A shop near 17th.

Rob:
What is it?

Lina:
Akuaba.

He nods, waiting.

Rob:
And?

Lina:
And nothing.
(beat)
I like looking at it.

Rob steps in, sits beside her.

Rob:
You look calmer.

Lina gives a small shrug.

Lina:
Maybe I’m tired.

He reaches for her hand. She lets him, but only briefly.

Rob:
Can we actually talk tonight?
About Tuesday. About all of it.

Lina keeps her eyes on the object.

Lina:
Not right now.

Rob:
There’s never a right now.

She stands, adjusts the akuaba angle by a few centimeters.

Lina:
I said not tonight.

Rob exhales, gets up.

Rob:
Okay.

He leaves the room. Lina remains, facing the dresser.

After a long beat, she whispers — unsure if to herself or the object.

Lina:
I just need one quiet week.

Scene 6: School Pressure + Lock-In Decision (Act 1 Break)

Interior SCHOOL STAFF ROOM — MORNING

Microwave hum. Coffee machine hiss. Bulletin board with event notices.

Lina enters with a container. Elena and two staff members are mid-conversation.

The tone shifts half a notch when Lina appears.

STAFF #1
Morning.

Elena:
Hey. Admin moved Tuesday PLC. You’ll still be there, right?

Lina opens the fridge.

Lina:
What time?

Elena:
Ten-thirty.

Lina freezes for a second.

Lina:
I have an appointment.

Elena:
With who?

Lina:
Medical.

Elena nods, trying to be practical.

Elena:
Then tell Singh now. She’s already edgy about absences this month.

Staff #2 jumps in, trying to lighten.

STAFF #2
Tell her it’s for a noble cause.

A tiny laugh from someone else. Lina doesn’t join.

Elena:
Ignore him.
(softer)
Do you want me to cover first ten minutes?

Lina looks at her. Then at the family-photo mug on the table. Then away.

Lina:
No. I’ll handle it.

Interior SCHOOL HALLWAY — CONTINUOUS

Lina stands outside ADMIN OFFICE door with her phone.

On screen: Clinic Confirmed — Tuesday 10:40

She opens text thread with clinic.

Types: Need to keep this slot. It’s important.
Deletes.

Types: Can you move me to next available after 4 PM?
Stops.

Deletes.

She opens a new note app instead:
- Tuesday 9:15 — class prep
- 10:30 — PLC
- 12:10 — parent call

She closes the note.

Opens text to clinic again.

Types: I can’t make Tuesday. Please cancel.

She stares at it. Sends.

Phone screen: Appointment canceled.

Lina locks phone, slips it into pocket.

She stands there, jaw tight, then heads toward class.
