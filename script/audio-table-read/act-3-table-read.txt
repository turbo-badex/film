ACT III

SCENE 19: PRE-HEARING CORRIDOR (PRESSURE CONVERGENCE)

INT. SCHOOL DISTRICT OFFICE — MONDAY MORNING

Neutral carpet. Fluorescent calm. Framed mission statements on the wall.

Lina sits outside Hearing Room B with a slim folder on her lap.

Across from her: Elena, uneasy but present. At the far end, Rob arrives, unsure if he should approach.

He does.

ROB
You want me in there?

Lina keeps eyes forward.

LINA
No.

ROB
I came anyway.

Beat.

ELENA
Lina... before this starts, just say what’s true. Not what sounds strongest.

Lina turns to Elena.

LINA
You don’t get to coach me now.

Elena absorbs it, nods once, steps back.

A district admin opens the hearing room door.

ADMIN
Ms. Morrison, we’re ready.

Lina stands. Rob meets her eyes.

ROB
I’m not against you.

Lina’s face flickers, then shuts.

LINA
That depends what you call support.

She enters. Door closes.

CUT TO:

SCENE 20: HEARING ROOM: BOUNDED TRUTH

INT. HEARING ROOM B — CONTINUOUS

Long table. Recorder light on. Singh, HR rep, district lead, union rep.

Lina sits alone on one side.

DISTRICT LEAD
We’ll keep this focused on conduct and student impact.

HR REP
You declined prior supports. Today we need your response on record.

Lina nods.

A printed packet slides toward her: parent complaints, observation notes, feedback samples.

Highlighted phrase: "teacher affect perceived as punitive."

SINGH
Did your personal stressors affect classroom communication?

Lina is silent a long moment.

LINA
Yes.

Small shift in room.

HR REP
Can you specify?

LINA
I was under pressure I didn’t handle well.
It narrowed how I spoke.

DISTRICT LEAD
Was this disclosed to administration at the time?

LINA
Not directly.

UNION REP
Do you want to add context now?

Lina looks at the packet, then up.

LINA
I confused control with professionalism.

The line lands.

SINGH
Do you acknowledge harm to students?

Lina swallows.

LINA
I acknowledge impact.

DISTRICT LEAD
And next steps?

Lina sees the prepared statement in front of her. Doesn’t read it.

LINA
I’ll take the support plan.
I should have before.

Another shift in room — relief mixed with consequence.

HR REP
Noted. We’ll proceed with conditional continuation and monitored return.

DISTRICT LEAD
There will still be formal notation for this period.

Lina nods. She expected that.

LINA
Understood.

Recorder light clicks off.

CUT TO:

SCENE 21: PARKING LOT CONFESSION (TOO LATE COST)

EXT. DISTRICT OFFICE PARKING LOT — LATE MORNING

Cold sun. Traffic hiss from nearby road.

Lina exits the building alone. Rob is waiting by his car.

He studies her face.

ROB
How bad?

LINA
Not fired.
(beat)
Not clean.

Rob nods.

Lina almost smiles. Doesn’t.

ROB
Did you tell yourself that?

Long beat.

Lina finally looks at him fully.

LINA
I was trying not to be the woman people pity.

Rob says nothing.

LINA (CONT'D)
If I kept everything precise, maybe nobody could see...

She can’t finish.

ROB
See what?

Lina exhales — this is the line she has avoided all film.

LINA
That I’m ashamed.

Silence.

ROB
Of what happened to us?

LINA
Of what didn’t happen.

Rob closes his eyes briefly. Pain, not surprise.

ROB
I needed that months ago.

Lina nods, tears held but not released.

LINA
I know.

ROB
I can’t step back into the old house because this sounded right one day.

Lina accepts it.

LINA
I’m not asking for that.

ROB
What are you asking?

Lina looks down at her hands. Empty.

LINA
I’m asking you not to decide this is all I am.

Rob takes that in.

ROB
I won’t.
(beat)
But I’m still leaving things as they are for now.

Lina nods.

He opens his car door, then stops.

ROB (CONT'D)
Are you going to be okay today?

Lina answers honestly for the first time.

LINA
No.
(beat)
But I think I can be responsible.

Rob nods once and gets in. Drives away.

Lina stands alone in the lot, steadying herself.

Phone buzzes: message from Elena — If you want soup, I can drop it at your door. No talking required.

Lina reads it. Doesn’t reply yet.

She starts walking.

SCENE 22: RETURN TO CLASSROOM (CHANGED BEHAVIOR, NOT SPEECHES)

INT. ELEMENTARY CLASSROOM — MORNING

Same room, same desks, softer energy.

Lina distributes revised writing assignments. Her tone is measured, less sharp.

LINA
Same standards. Different process.
If you make a strong point, I’ll ask for evidence.
If you get stuck, I’ll ask a better question.

Maya (from Seq 6) watches Lina carefully.

MAYA
So... we can still be wrong?

A few kids snicker.

LINA
Please be wrong.
That means you’re trying.

Lina moves desk to desk during writing time.

On one paper she writes in red:
- "Good risk"
- "Be specific"

She pauses, then adds in smaller script:
- "Don’t hide your claim."

She doesn’t cross it out this time.

At the board she accidentally reaches for a black marker, then changes to red. Holds it for a beat, then sets it down and keeps teaching without writing anything.

No one comments. The room breathes.

CUT TO:

SCENE 23: THRESHOLD ENCOUNTER WITH ROB (NOT RECONCILED)

EXT. APARTMENT BUILDING ENTRY — DUSK

Snowmelt in gutters. Dim porch light.

Lina arrives with groceries and sees Rob by the doorway holding a storage box.

Inside: books, a mug, clinic paperwork.

ROB
These were still at my sister’s.
Thought you’d want them.

Lina takes the box.

LINA
Thanks.

ROB
How was school?

LINA
Quieter.
(beat)
Still hard.

ROB
That tracks.

A small silence.

ROB (CONT'D)
I heard you changed how you’re giving feedback.

LINA
I’m trying to hear myself before they have to.

He nods. No victory in it.

ROB
I’m not ready to come back.

LINA
I know.

ROB
And I can’t pretend the last months didn’t happen.

LINA
You shouldn’t.

ROB
I’m not gone either.
Just... not there yet.

Lina absorbs it. No bargaining.

LINA
Okay.

He points at the grocery bag.

ROB
Need a hand?

LINA
I’ve got it.

ROB
Goodnight, Lina.

LINA
Night.

He walks off.

Lina watches him go, then goes inside with both the bag and the box.

CUT TO:

SCENE 24: AMBIGUOUS CODA (OBJECT UNRESOLVED, COST CLEAR)

INT. APARTMENT BEDROOM — NIGHT

Low lamp. Quiet.

Lina opens the box on the bed.

Inside: clinic forms, old sonogram pamphlet, a photo strip of her and Rob laughing years ago.

She places the photo strip on the dresser.

The akuaba sits nearby.

She picks it up. Wipes dust from the face with her thumb.

She sets it down.

Then moves it off-center.

Not hidden. Not enthroned.

She opens a notebook and writes:

"Truth without cruelty."

She stares at the line. Doesn’t add a second one.

Her phone buzzes.
Unknown parent message:
"Maya came home proud today. Thank you for seeing her."

Lina reads it twice.

She looks at the akuaba.

Long, unreadable gaze.

Faith? Habit? Farewell? Need?

She reaches toward it -- stops -- leaves it where it is.

She turns off the lamp.

City spill from the window holds Lina and the object in the same frame, with distance between them.

Hold.

Cut to black.
