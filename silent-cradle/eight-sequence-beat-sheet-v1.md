# Silent Cradle — 8-Sequence Beat Sheet (v1)

Date: 2026-02-22  
Built from: `three-act-shell-v1.md` + lore bible + Farhadi pattern framework  
Target runtime: ~90 min

## How to use this
Each sequence is a writing guardrail. Any scene we draft should do at least one of these jobs:  
1) raise pressure, 2) force a choice, 3) impose cost, 4) shift relationship power.

---

## Sequence 1 (Act 1A) — The functional mask
**Runtime target:** min 1–12  
**Dramatic job:** Establish Lina’s external competence vs internal wound.

- **Opening world:** school routine, controlled classroom authority, red-pen precision.
- **Private contrast:** home with Rob feels emotionally procedural, not intimate.
- **Pressure source:** subtle peer/institution cues around motherhood and life milestones.
- **Key choice:** Lina chooses performance (composure/control) over vulnerability.
- **Sequence cost:** emotional distance from Rob is normalized.
- **Hand-off:** we feel the wound, but Lina can still pass as “fine.”

---

## Sequence 2 (Act 1B) — Object attachment and lock-in
**Runtime target:** min 12–25  
**Dramatic job:** Introduce akuaba as coping ritual and cross first threshold.

- **Entry point:** Lina encounters Kwame / object context (grounded, respectful, no mysticism shortcuts).
- **Early relief:** ritual gives temporary emotional regulation and private meaning.
- **Rob dynamic:** he tolerates/supports coping but avoids deeper grief talk.
- **Key choice:** Lina invests identity into ritual rather than relational repair.
- **Act 1 break:** she makes a consequential decision that prioritizes object-mediated control.
- **Sequence cost:** first missed opportunity for honest repair with Rob.

---

## Sequence 3 (Act 2A) — Expansion of control
**Runtime target:** min 25–38  
**Dramatic job:** Move from private coping to behavior-shaping compulsion.

- **School bleed-through:** classroom conduct and grading tone reflect Lina’s internal rigidity.
- **Symbol channel:** red-pen comments begin mirroring private conflict.
- **Peer pressure:** colleague concern emerges as soft surveillance.
- **Key choice:** Lina doubles down on control when offered support.
- **Sequence cost:** trust starts eroding at home and work.
- **Hand-off:** behavior is now observable by others.

---

## Sequence 4 (Act 2B) — Midpoint moral pivot (declined repair)
**Runtime target:** min 38–50  
**Dramatic job:** Offer a credible path to repair; Lina rejects it.

- **Repair opening:** Dr. Chen / Rob / Elena creates a concrete chance for honest reset.
- **Moral split:** truth + vulnerability vs image + control.
- **Key choice (midpoint):** Lina chooses image protection.
- **Midpoint consequence:** story objective flips from “healing possible” to “what will this cost?”
- **Sequence cost:** relational fracture becomes active and directional.

---

## Sequence 5 (Act 2C) — Social exposure machinery
**Runtime target:** min 50–62  
**Dramatic job:** Activate institutional and reputational stakes.

- **Institution pressure:** school/admin framing of concern, optics, professionalism.
- **Peer pressure:** friend intervention escalates from care to overreach.
- **Conflicting versions:** events are interpreted differently by Lina/Rob/peers.
- **Key choice:** Lina counters scrutiny with tighter narrative control.
- **Sequence cost:** greater public risk; fewer private exits.
- **Hand-off:** her anti-shame strategy is producing more shame.

---

## Sequence 6 (Act 2D) — The dark turn
**Runtime target:** min 62–72  
**Dramatic job:** Corner Lina morally and relationally before climax.

- **Compounded pressure:** home rupture + institutional stakes + peer fallout.
- **Threshold event:** a confrontation or decision that cannot be undone.
- **Key choice:** Lina escalates instead of surrendering control.
- **End of Act 2 state:** she risks losing marriage, standing, and self-trust.
- **Sequence cost:** irreversible damage enters the story.

---

## Sequence 7 (Act 3A) — Reckoning confrontation
**Runtime target:** min 72–84  
**Dramatic job:** Force explicit naming of what Lina has defended.

- **Convergence scene:** all forces collide (Rob, peer/institution, Lina’s self-image).
- **Truth pressure:** Lina must articulate core wound: childlessness shame + ego defense.
- **Key choice:** bounded truth over total denial (but not full cleansing confession).
- **Sequence cost:** truth arrives too late to prevent major consequences.
- **Hand-off:** outcome depends on whether connection is still possible after damage.

---

## Sequence 8 (Act 3B) — Ambiguous coda, clear lesson
**Runtime target:** min 84–90  
**Dramatic job:** End with moral clarity and spiritual ambiguity.

- **Aftermath texture:** quieter behavior changes, no speechifying.
- **Object ambiguity:** Lina’s relationship to akuaba remains open (faith, symbolism, or unresolved dependency).
- **Relational clarity:** audience sees the cost of choosing ego/image over relationship.
- **Final beat:** imperfect forward motion, not neat reconciliation.

---

## Motif progression map (cross-sequence)
- **Red pen:** correction -> projection -> hesitation -> guidance.
- **Doors/thresholds:** avoidance -> confrontation -> partial opening.
- **Routine actions:** competence mask -> stress leakage -> slowed intentionality.

---

## Scene-writing checklist (for next pass)
- [ ] Scene starts in ordinary task/routine.
- [ ] Hidden moral objective exists beneath surface dialogue.
- [ ] Lina makes a choice (even if small).
- [ ] Choice has immediate cost.
- [ ] Motif state advances (not repeated flat).
- [ ] End on pressure, not explanation.
